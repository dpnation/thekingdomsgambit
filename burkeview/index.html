The Apartment

Your own space, at the base of the mountain.

Burke View is situated at the base of Burke Mountain. Kingdom Trails are accessible directly from the property. And you can be on Burke Mountain's chairlift in literally a few minutes.

The space is a 1-bedroom/1 bath apartment with a fully equipped kitchen and open living area with gorgeous views of Burke Mountain. The bedroom has a king sized bed. The bathroom is en suite off of the bedroom with a stand up shower. The kitchen and living room are open-concept with a queen sofa bed. Sleeps 2 very comfortably and up to 4 utilizing the pull out.
